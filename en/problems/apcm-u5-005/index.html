AP Physics C: Mechanics · Torque and Rotational Dynamics · Step 5

A 0.50 kg particle is at position $(4.0,\,3.0)$ m in the $xy$-plane, moving with velocity $(2.0,\,-1.0)$ m/s. What is its angular momentum $L_z$ about the origin, in kg m$^2$/s?

  1. $-5.0$
  2. $+5.0$
  3. $+2.5$
  4. $-10.0$
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Angular momentum is $\vec r\times m\vec v$, not a dot product.

Hint 2. For motion in the plane, $L_z=m(xv_y-yv_x)$.

Hint 3. $L_z=0.50\,[(4.0)(-1.0)-(3.0)(2.0)]$.

Solution

$L_z=m(xv_y-yv_x)=0.50[(4.0)(-1.0)-(3.0)(2.0)]=0.50(-10)=-5.0$ kg m$^2$/s (clockwise). The $+2.5$ choice comes from a dot product, and $-10$ forgets the mass. The correct choice is A.

Answer: A. $-5.0$
