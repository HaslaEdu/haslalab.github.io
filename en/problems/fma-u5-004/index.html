F=ma · Rotation and Angular Momentum — practice problem 24

F=ma · Rotation and Angular Momentum · Step 6

Blocks of mass 3 kg and 1 kg hang from the two ends of a string that passes over a pulley, a uniform disk of mass 4 kg and radius $R$ that turns on a fixed frictionless axle. The string does not slip on the pulley. Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$. What is the magnitude of the blocks' acceleration?

  1. 5.0 m/s$^2$
  2. 2.0 m/s$^2$
  3. 3.3 m/s$^2$
  4. 4.0 m/s$^2$
  5. 2.5 m/s$^2$
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. The two string tensions differ, because the pulley has a moment of inertia.

Hint 2. The pulley's rotational inertia acts like an extra mass $I/R^2$ in the linear equation.

Hint 3. $(3-1)g=(3+1+I/R^2)a$ with $I=\tfrac12MR^2$.

Solution

The net driving force is $(3-1)(10)=20$ N. The effective inertia is $3+1+\tfrac12(4)=6$ kg, so $a=20/6\approx3.3$ m/s$^2$. (5.0 would be the answer for a massless pulley.)

Answer: C. 3.3 m/s$^2$
