USAPhO · Waves and Optics · Step 6

A car drives at $20$ m/s straight toward a large flat cliff face, sounding its horn, which emits $400$ Hz. The driver hears the horn directly (at $400$ Hz, since the horn moves with the driver) and also its echo from the cliff. What beat frequency does the driver hear? Take the speed of sound in still air as $343$ m/s, with no wind. Give the answer in Hz.

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Treat the cliff as a stationary observer that re-emits the frequency it receives.

Hint 2. The cliff receives $f\frac{c}{c-u}$; the driver, moving toward the cliff, hears $f'\frac{c+u}{c}$.

Hint 3. $f_{echo}=f\frac{c+u}{c-u}$; the beat frequency is $f_{echo}-f$.

Solution

The stationary cliff receives $f_1=f\,c/(c-u)$ from the moving source, and the approaching driver hears $f_2=f_1(c+u)/c=f\frac{c+u}{c-u}$. The beat frequency is $f_2-f=\frac{2u}{c-u}f=\frac{40}{323}(400)\approx49.5$ Hz.

Answer: 49.54 Hz
