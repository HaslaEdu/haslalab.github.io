A $24$ V battery of negligible internal resistance is connected in series with a $3\,\Omega$ resistor, and this series combination feeds a parallel pair of resistors of $4\,\Omega$ and $12\,\Omega$. Find the current through the $4\,\Omega$ resistor. Answer as an integer in amperes.

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Find the parallel equivalent first.

Hint 2. $R_{par}=\dfrac{4\times12}{16}=3\,\Omega$, so total resistance is $6\,\Omega$.

Hint 3. Current divides inversely to resistance: the $4\,\Omega$ branch takes $12/16$ of the total.

Solution

$R_{par}=3\,\Omega$, $R_{tot}=6\,\Omega$, $I=24/6=4$ A. The $4\,\Omega$ branch gets $\dfrac{12}{12+4}\times4=3$ A.

Answer: 3 A
