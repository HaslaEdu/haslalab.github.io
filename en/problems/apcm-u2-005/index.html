AP Physics C: Mechanics · Force and Translational Dynamics · Step 5

A 4.0 kg block rests on a horizontal table with kinetic friction coefficient 0.25. A light string runs from it over a frictionless pulley to a 6.0 kg block hanging freely. After release, what is the acceleration of the blocks? Use $g=9.8$ m/s$^2$.

  1. $5.88$ m/s$^2$
  2. $3.43$ m/s$^2$
  3. $4.9$ m/s$^2$
  4. $4.41$ m/s$^2$
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Friction acts only on the block on the table, with normal force equal to its own weight.

Hint 2. Treat the two blocks as one system along the string: net force $=m_2g-\mu m_1g$.

Hint 3. $a=(m_2g-\mu m_1g)/(m_1+m_2)$.

Solution

The friction force is $\mu m_1g=0.25(4.0)(9.8)=9.8$ N. The net driving force is $6.0(9.8)-9.8=49$ N on a total mass of 10 kg, so $a=4.9$ m/s$^2$. The correct choice is C.

Answer: C. $4.9$ m/s$^2$
