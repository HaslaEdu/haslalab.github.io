AP Physics 1 · Torque and Rotational Dynamics · Step 4

A solid disc and a thin hoop have the same mass and the same radius. Each is free to rotate about its central axis and the same constant net torque is applied to each, starting from rest. Which statement is correct?

  1. The hoop has the larger angular acceleration because more of its mass is far from the axis.
  2. Both have the same angular acceleration because they have the same mass and radius.
  3. The disc has the larger angular acceleration because its mass is larger.
  4. The disc has the larger angular acceleration because its rotational inertia is smaller.
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Use the rotational form of Newton's second law.

Hint 2. Compare $I=\tfrac12MR^2$ and $I=MR^2$.

Hint 3. $\alpha=\tau_{net}/I$.

Solution

Rotational dynamics gives $\alpha=\tau_{net}/I$. The disc has $I=\tfrac12MR^2$ and the hoop has $I=MR^2$, so for the same torque the disc has twice the angular acceleration. Mass farther from the axis increases rotational inertia and makes spinning harder.

Answer: D. The disc has the larger angular acceleration because its rotational inertia is smaller.
