F=ma · Oscillations · Step 6

A simple pendulum has period $T_0$ when its support is at rest. It is then hung in a cart that accelerates horizontally at $a=\tfrac34g$. For small oscillations about the new equilibrium, what is the period in terms of $T_0$?

  1. $0.89\,T_0$
  2. $0.64\,T_0$
  3. $0.71\,T_0$
  4. $0.80\,T_0$
  5. $1.12\,T_0$
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. In the cart's frame there is an extra, fictitious force.

Hint 2. Combine it with gravity to get an effective gravitational acceleration.

Hint 3. $g_{\text{eff}}=\sqrt{g^2+a^2}$ and $T\propto g_{\text{eff}}^{-1/2}$.

Solution

In the cart's frame $g_{\text{eff}}=\sqrt{g^2+(0.75g)^2}=1.25g$. Since $T\propto1/\sqrt{g_{\text{eff}}}$, $T=T_0/\sqrt{1.25}\approx0.89\,T_0$. (0.80 would forget the square root.)

Answer: A. $0.89\,T_0$
