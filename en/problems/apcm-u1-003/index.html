AP Physics C: Mechanics · Kinematics · Step 4

A projectile is launched from level ground with speed $v_0$ at angle $\theta$ above the horizontal, ignoring air resistance. Its horizontal range $R$ turns out to be exactly four times its maximum height $H$. What is $\theta$?

  1. $30^\circ$
  2. $53^\circ$
  3. $60^\circ$
  4. $45^\circ$
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Write $R$ and $H$ in terms of $v_0$ and $\theta$ and take the ratio.

Hint 2. $R=v_0^2\sin2\theta/g$ and $H=v_0^2\sin^2\theta/(2g)$.

Hint 3. $R/H=4\cot\theta$; set it equal to 4.

Solution

$R=\dfrac{v_0^2\sin2\theta}{g}$, $H=\dfrac{v_0^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}$, so $R/H=\dfrac{2\sin2\theta}{\sin^2\theta}=4\cot\theta$. Setting $R/H=4$ gives $\cot\theta=1$, so $\theta=45^\circ$. The correct choice is D.

Answer: D. $45^\circ$
