F=ma · Dynamics and Friction — practice problem 9

F=ma · Dynamics and Friction · Step 5

Inside an elevator accelerating upward at $2\ \text{m/s}^2$, a 2 kg block hangs from the ceiling by a string, and a 3 kg block hangs from the 2 kg block by a second string. Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$. What is the tension in the lower string (between the two blocks)?

  1. 60 N
  2. 36 N
  3. 24 N
  4. 30 N
  5. 12 N
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Which blocks does the lower string have to accelerate?

Hint 2. Only the 3 kg block hangs from the lower string.

Hint 3. $T-m g=m a$ with $a=+2$ upward.

Solution

For the 3 kg block: $T-30=3(2)$, so $T=36$ N. (The upper string carries $5\cdot12=60$ N; 30 N would be the tension without the elevator's acceleration.)

Answer: B. 36 N
