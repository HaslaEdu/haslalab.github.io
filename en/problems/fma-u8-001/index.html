F=ma · Statics, Fluids and Estimation · Step 6

A uniform ladder of mass 20 kg leans against a smooth vertical wall, with its foot on rough ground. The ladder makes an angle $\theta$ with the floor, with $\tan\theta=2$. Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$. What friction force must the ground exert on the ladder to keep it from slipping?

  1. 200 N
  2. 25 N
  3. 75 N
  4. 100 N
  5. 50 N
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Take torques about the foot of the ladder to find the wall's force.

Hint 2. Then apply horizontal force balance.

Hint 3. Wall force $N_w\,L\sin\theta=mg\,(L/2)\cos\theta$.

Solution

Torques about the foot: $N_wL\sin\theta=mg\tfrac L2\cos\theta$, so $N_w=\dfrac{mg}{2\tan\theta}=\dfrac{200}{4}=50$ N. The wall is smooth, so horizontal balance requires friction $f=N_w=50$ N.

Answer: E. 50 N
