F=ma · Rotation and Angular Momentum · Step 6

A frictionless turntable of moment of inertia $20\ \text{kg m}^2$ spins freely. A 40 kg person stands on it at 1.0 m from the axis, and the two rotate together. The person then walks slowly to the axis (treat the person as a point mass). What is the ratio (final kinetic energy)/(initial kinetic energy)?

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. 9
  5. 6
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. What is conserved when the person walks inward, and what is not?

Hint 2. Kinetic energy can be written as $L^2/(2I)$.

Hint 3. Initial $I=20+40(1)^2$, final $I=20$.

Solution

Angular momentum is conserved. With $K=L^2/2I$, $K_f/K_i=I_i/I_f=(20+40)/20=3$. The extra energy comes from the work the person does walking inward against the (fictitious) outward push. (9 would result from squaring the ratio of $\omega$.)

Answer: C. 3
