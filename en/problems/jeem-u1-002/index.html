JEE Main · Units and Measurements · Step 7

A length is constructed from the gravitational constant $G$, Planck's constant $h$ and the speed of light $c$ as $L\propto G^{x}h^{y}c^{z}$. The exponents $(x,y,z)$ are:

  1. $(\tfrac12,\tfrac12,-\tfrac32)$
  2. $(\tfrac12,\tfrac12,-\tfrac12)$
  3. $(-\tfrac12,\tfrac12,\tfrac32)$
  4. $(1,1,-3)$
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Write the dimensions of $G$, $h$ and $c$ in terms of $M$, $L$, $T$.

Hint 2. $[G]=M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}$, $[h]=ML^2T^{-1}$, $[c]=LT^{-1}$. Equate powers of $M$, $L$, $T$ to those of $L^1$.

Hint 3. Mass: $-x+y=0$. Time: $-2x-y-z=0$. Length: $3x+2y+z=1$.

Solution

Mass: $-x+y=0\Rightarrow y=x$. Time: $-2x-y-z=0\Rightarrow z=-3x$. Length: $3x+2y+z=1\Rightarrow 3x+2x-3x=1\Rightarrow x=\tfrac12$. So $y=\tfrac12$, $z=-\tfrac32$.

Answer: A. $(\tfrac12,\tfrac12,-\tfrac32)$
