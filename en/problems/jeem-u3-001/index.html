Masses $3$ kg and $5$ kg are connected by a light string passing over a smooth, light, fixed pulley. They are released from rest. Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$. The tension in the string is:

  1. 30 N
  2. 40 N
  3. 50 N
  4. 37.5 N
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Write Newton's second law separately for each mass.

Hint 2. The acceleration is $a=\dfrac{(5-3)g}{5+3}$.

Hint 3. $T=m_1(g+a)$ for the lighter mass.

Solution

$a=\dfrac{(5-3)(10)}{8}=2.5$ m/s$^2$. For the 3 kg mass, $T-30=3(2.5)\Rightarrow T=37.5$ N.

Answer: D. 37.5 N
