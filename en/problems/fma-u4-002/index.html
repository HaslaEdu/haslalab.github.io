F=ma · Momentum and Collisions · Step 5

A 20 g bullet embeds itself in a 1.98 kg wooden block hanging from long light strings. The block-plus-bullet system swings up to a maximum height 0.20 m above its starting position. Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$. What was the bullet's speed just before impact?

  1. 200 m/s
  2. 20 m/s
  3. 100 m/s
  4. 400 m/s
  5. 198 m/s
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Split the problem into a very short collision and a later swing.

Hint 2. Momentum is conserved in the collision, energy in the swing.

Hint 3. $v_{\text{after}}=\sqrt{2gh}$ and $m v=(m+M)v_{\text{after}}$.

Solution

After the collision $v'=\sqrt{2\cdot10\cdot0.2}=2$ m/s. Momentum: $0.02\,v=2.00\cdot2$, so $v=200$ m/s. (198 m/s comes from using only the block's mass.)

Answer: A. 200 m/s
