F=ma · Oscillations · Step 6

A flat horizontal platform oscillates horizontally in simple harmonic motion with period $\pi/5$ s. A small block rests on it, with a coefficient of static friction 0.40 between them. Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$. What is the largest amplitude for which the block does not slip?

  1. 0.020 m
  2. 0.10 m
  3. 0.40 m
  4. 0.0040 m
  5. 0.040 m
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. The block needs a maximum acceleration supplied by friction.

Hint 2. The platform's maximum acceleration is $\omega^2A$.

Hint 3. $\omega^2A\le\mu_s g$, with $\omega=2\pi/T$.

Solution

$\omega=2\pi/(\pi/5)=10$ rad/s. Slipping starts when $\omega^2A=\mu_s g$, i.e. $A=\dfrac{0.4\cdot10}{100}=0.040$ m.

Answer: E. 0.040 m
