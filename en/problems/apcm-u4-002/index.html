AP Physics C: Mechanics · Linear Momentum · Step 5

Sand drops vertically onto a horizontal conveyor belt at a steady rate of 5.0 kg/s. The belt moves at constant speed 2.0 m/s. What power must the motor supply to keep the belt at constant speed (beyond friction in the belt itself)? Answer in watts.

Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. The sand gains horizontal momentum, so the belt must exert a horizontal force on it.

Hint 2. $F=\dfrac{dp}{dt}=v\,\dfrac{dm}{dt}$ since $v$ is constant.

Hint 3. $P=Fv=v^2\,dm/dt$.

Solution

$F=v\,\dfrac{dm}{dt}=(2.0)(5.0)=10$ N. $P=Fv=10\times2.0=20$ W. Only half of this ($\tfrac12v^2\,dm/dt=10$ W) becomes kinetic energy of the sand; the rest is dissipated as the sand slips onto the belt.

Answer: 20 W
