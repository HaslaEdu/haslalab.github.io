A particle moves along a straight line with position $x=t^{3}-6t^{2}+9t$ (SI units). Find the total distance travelled in the first $4$ s. Answer as an integer in metres.

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Distance is not the same as displacement if the particle turns around.

Hint 2. Find when $v=0$ and split the motion there.

Hint 3. $v=3t^2-12t+9$ vanishes at $t=1$ s and $t=3$ s; add $|\Delta x|$ for each interval.

Solution

$v=3(t-1)(t-3)$, so the particle reverses at $t=1$ and $t=3$ s. $x(0)=0$, $x(1)=4$, $x(3)=0$, $x(4)=4$. Distance $=4+4+4=12$ m.

Answer: 12 m
