AP Physics 2 · Thermodynamics · Step 3

Samples of helium (molar mass 4.0 g/mol) and oxygen gas (O$_2$, molar mass 32 g/mol) are in thermal equilibrium at the same temperature. Which statement is correct?

  1. The helium molecules have the greater average kinetic energy and the greater rms speed.
  2. The molecules have the same average translational kinetic energy, and the helium rms speed is about 2.8 times the oxygen rms speed.
  3. The molecules have the same average translational kinetic energy, and the helium rms speed is 8 times the oxygen rms speed.
  4. The molecules have the same rms speed and the same average kinetic energy.
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. What does temperature measure for an ideal gas at the molecular level?

Hint 2. Average translational KE is $\tfrac{3}{2}k_BT$, independent of the gas.

Hint 3. Since $\tfrac12 m v_{rms}^2$ is equal, $v_{rms}\propto 1/\sqrt{m}$.

Solution

Average translational kinetic energy per molecule is $\tfrac32 k_BT$, the same for any ideal gas at the same $T$. Setting $\tfrac12 m_{He}v_{He}^2 = \tfrac12 m_{O}v_{O}^2$ gives $v_{He}/v_{O} = \sqrt{m_O/m_{He}} = \sqrt{8} \approx 2.8$. The factor 8 is the mass ratio, not the speed ratio.

Answer: B. The molecules have the same average translational kinetic energy, and the helium rms speed is about 2.8 times the oxygen rms speed.
