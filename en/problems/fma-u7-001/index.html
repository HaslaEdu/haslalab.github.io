F=ma · Oscillations · Step 5

A 0.50 kg block on a horizontal frictionless surface is attached to a spring of constant 50 N/m and oscillates with amplitude 0.10 m. What is its speed when it is 0.060 m from the equilibrium position?

  1. 0.80 m/s
  2. 1.0 m/s
  3. 0.60 m/s
  4. 0.48 m/s
  5. 1.2 m/s
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Use energy conservation, or $v=\omega\sqrt{A^2-x^2}$.

Hint 2. Find $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}$ first.

Hint 3. $\omega=10$ rad/s.

Solution

$\omega=\sqrt{50/0.5}=10$ rad/s. Then $v=\omega\sqrt{A^2-x^2}=10\sqrt{0.01-0.0036}=10(0.08)=0.80$ m/s. (1.0 m/s is the maximum speed $\omega A$; 0.60 m/s is $\omega x$.)

Answer: A. 0.80 m/s
