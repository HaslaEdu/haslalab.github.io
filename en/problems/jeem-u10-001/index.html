JEE Main · Oscillations and Waves · Step 5

A simple pendulum has period $T$ in a stationary lift. The lift now accelerates upward at $g/3$. The new period is:

  1. $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}T$
  2. $\dfrac{3}{4}T$
  3. $\dfrac{T}{\sqrt3}$
  4. $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}T$
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Effective gravity changes in an accelerating frame.

Hint 2. Upward acceleration $a$ gives $g_{eff}=g+a$.

Hint 3. $T'=2\pi\sqrt{L/g_{eff}}$ with $g_{eff}=\tfrac43g$.

Solution

$g_{eff}=g+g/3=\tfrac43g$. $T'/T=\sqrt{g/g_{eff}}=\sqrt{3/4}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

Answer: D. $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}T$
