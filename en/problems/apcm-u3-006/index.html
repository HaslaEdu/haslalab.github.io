AP Physics C: Mechanics · Work, Energy, and Power · Step 7

A small block sits at the top of a fixed, frictionless hemisphere of radius 1.5 m and is given a negligible nudge so it slides off. Using $g=9.8$ m/s$^2$, find its speed at the instant it leaves the surface of the hemisphere. Answer in m/s.

Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. The block leaves when the normal force becomes zero.

Hint 2. Combine energy conservation $v^2=2gR(1-\cos\theta)$ with the radial equation $mg\cos\theta-N=mv^2/R$.

Hint 3. Setting $N=0$ gives $v^2=gR\cos\theta$, which together with energy gives $\cos\theta=2/3$.

Solution

Energy: $v^2=2gR(1-\cos\theta)$. Radial: $mg\cos\theta-N=mv^2/R$, with $N=0$ at departure so $v^2=gR\cos\theta$. Equating gives $\cos\theta=2/3$, hence $v=\sqrt{gR\cdot\tfrac23}=\sqrt{9.8}\approx3.13$ m/s.

Answer: 3.1305 m/s
