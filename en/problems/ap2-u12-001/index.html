AP Physics 2 · Magnetism and Electromagnetism · Step 4

A proton ($m = 1.67\times10^{-27}$ kg, $q = 1.60\times10^{-19}$ C) moves at $2.0\times10^{6}$ m/s perpendicular to a uniform 0.50 T magnetic field, so it travels in a circle. What is the radius of the circle, in centimeters?

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. What force provides the centripetal force?

Hint 2. Set the magnetic force equal to $mv^2/r$.

Hint 3. $qvB = mv^2/r \Rightarrow r = mv/(qB)$.

Solution

The magnetic force $qvB$ supplies the centripetal force $mv^2/r$, giving $r = mv/(qB)$. Then $r = (1.67\times10^{-27})(2.0\times10^6)/\left[(1.60\times10^{-19})(0.50)\right] = 0.0418\ \text{m} = 4.18\ \text{cm}$.

Answer: 4.175 cm
