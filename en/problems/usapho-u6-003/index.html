USAPhO · Modern Physics — practice problem 28

USAPhO · Modern Physics · Step 7

A proton beam strikes protons at rest in a hydrogen target. What is the minimum kinetic energy of a beam proton for the reaction $p+p\to p+p+\pi^0$ to be possible? Use $m_pc^2=938.27$ MeV and $m_{\pi^0}c^2=134.98$ MeV. Give the answer in MeV.

Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. At threshold all final particles move together, i.e. they are at rest in the centre-of-momentum frame.

Hint 2. The invariant $s=(E_{tot})^2-(p_{tot}c)^2$ is the same in all frames. In the lab, $s=2m_p^2c^4+2E_1m_pc^2$.

Hint 3. Set $s=(2m_p+m_\pi)^2c^4$ and solve for $K=E_1-m_pc^2$.

Solution

In the lab $s=(E_1+m_pc^2)^2-(p_1c)^2=2m_p^2c^4+2E_1m_pc^2$. At threshold $s=(2m_p+m_\pi)^2c^4$, so $K_{th}=E_1-m_pc^2=\frac{(2m_p+m_\pi)^2-4m_p^2}{2m_p}c^2=m_\pi c^2\left(2+\frac{m_\pi}{2m_p}\right)\approx279.7$ MeV, about twice the pion rest energy.

Answer: 279.7 MeV
