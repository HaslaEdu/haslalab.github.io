F=ma · Kinematics — practice problem 2

F=ma · Kinematics · Step 5

A particle starts from rest at $t=0$ and moves along a straight line with acceleration $a=ct$, where $c$ is a constant. Let $d_1$ be the distance it covers between $t=0$ and $t=1$ s, and $d_2$ the distance between $t=1$ s and $t=2$ s. What is $d_2/d_1$?

  1. 3
  2. 5
  3. 7
  4. 4
  5. 8
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Integrate the acceleration twice, using the starting conditions.

Hint 2. The position grows like a power of $t$; find which power.

Hint 3. Compute $x(1)$, $x(2)$ and form $(x(2)-x(1))/x(1)$.

Solution

From $a=ct$ and rest at $t=0$: $v=ct^2/2$ and $x=ct^3/6$. Then $d_1=c/6$ and $d_2=c(8-1)/6=7c/6$. So $d_2/d_1=7$. (The answer 3 would hold for constant acceleration, and 8 is $x(2)/x(1)$.)

Answer: C. 7
