AP Physics C: Mechanics · Kinematics · Step 5

A particle moving along a line has velocity $v(t)=3t^2-12t$ in m/s. What total distance does it travel between $t=0$ and $t=5$ s?

  1. $25$ m
  2. $39$ m
  3. $7$ m
  4. $57$ m
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Distance and displacement differ if the velocity changes sign.

Hint 2. Find when $v=0$ and integrate $|v|$ piece by piece.

Hint 3. $x(t)-x(0)=t^3-6t^2$; $v<0$ for $0<t<4$ and $v>0$ for $4<t<5$.

Solution

$\Delta x(t)=t^3-6t^2$. The velocity is negative until $t=4$ s: displacement $-32$ m, so distance $32$ m. From 4 s to 5 s, $\Delta x=-25-(-32)=+7$ m. Total distance $=32+7=39$ m (the net displacement is only $-25$ m).

Answer: B. $39$ m
