F=ma · Energy and Work — practice problem 14

F=ma · Energy and Work · Step 6

A pendulum bob of mass $m$ hangs from a light string of length $L$. It is released from rest with the string making an angle of $60^\circ$ to the vertical. What is the string tension when the bob passes the lowest point?

  1. $1.5\,mg$
  2. $2.5\,mg$
  3. $2mg$
  4. $mg$
  5. $3mg$
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Energy conservation gives the speed at the bottom.

Hint 2. At the bottom the net upward force provides the centripetal force.

Hint 3. $v^2=2gL(1-\cos60^\circ)$ and $T-mg=mv^2/L$.

Solution

The bob drops $L(1-\cos60^\circ)=L/2$, so $v^2=gL$. At the bottom $T-mg=mv^2/L=mg$, giving $T=2mg$. (3mg is the result for release from the horizontal.)

Answer: C. $2mg$
