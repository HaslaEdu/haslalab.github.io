AP Physics C: Mechanics · Linear Momentum · Step 5

A thin rod of length $L$ has linear mass density $\lambda(x)=kx$, increasing from zero at the end $x=0$ to its largest value at $x=L$. Where is its center of mass measured from $x=0$?

  1. $\dfrac L2$
  2. $\dfrac L3$
  3. $\dfrac{2L}{3}$
  4. $\dfrac{3L}{4}$
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Use $x_{cm}=\frac1M\int x\,dm$ with $dm=\lambda\,dx$.

Hint 2. $M=\int_0^L kx\,dx=kL^2/2$.

Hint 3. $\int_0^L x(kx)\,dx=kL^3/3$.

Solution

$M=\int_0^L kx\,dx=\dfrac{kL^2}{2}$ and $\int x\,dm=\int_0^L kx^2\,dx=\dfrac{kL^3}{3}$. So $x_{cm}=\dfrac{kL^3/3}{kL^2/2}=\dfrac{2L}{3}$. The correct choice is C.

Answer: C. $\dfrac{2L}{3}$
