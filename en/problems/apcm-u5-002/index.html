AP Physics C: Mechanics · Torque and Rotational Dynamics · Step 5

Two blocks of mass 2.0 kg and 3.0 kg hang from a light string that passes over a pulley, which is a uniform solid disk of mass 4.0 kg and radius 0.10 m. The string does not slip and the axle is frictionless. Using $g=9.8$ m/s$^2$, find the acceleration of the blocks. Answer in m/s$^2$.

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. The tensions on the two sides of the pulley are different, because the pulley has rotational inertia.

Hint 2. Apply $\tau=I\alpha$ to the pulley with $\alpha=a/R$ and $I=\tfrac12MR^2$.

Hint 3. $a=\dfrac{(m_2-m_1)g}{m_1+m_2+M/2}$.

Solution

Blocks: $m_2g-T_2=m_2a$, $T_1-m_1g=m_1a$. Pulley: $(T_2-T_1)R=\tfrac12MR^2(a/R)$, so $T_2-T_1=\tfrac12Ma$. Adding: $(m_2-m_1)g=(m_1+m_2+\tfrac M2)a$, giving $a=\dfrac{9.8}{2+3+2}=1.4$ m/s$^2$.

Answer: 1.4 m/s^2
