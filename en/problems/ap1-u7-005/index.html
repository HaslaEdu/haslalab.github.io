AP Physics 1 · Oscillations · Step 5

An ideal mass-spring system oscillates with amplitude $A$. The system is restarted with the amplitude doubled to $2A$ (same mass and spring). Which pair correctly describes the new period and total mechanical energy compared with the originals?

  1. The period is unchanged and the total energy doubles.
  2. The period doubles and the total energy doubles.
  3. The period doubles and the total energy is four times larger.
  4. The period is unchanged and the total energy is four times larger.
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Does the period depend on amplitude for a mass-spring system?

Hint 2. Total energy equals the spring energy at maximum displacement.

Hint 3. $E=\tfrac12kA^2$ and $T=2\pi\sqrt{m/k}$.

Solution

The period $T=2\pi\sqrt{m/k}$ does not depend on amplitude. The total energy is $E=\tfrac12kA^2$, so doubling $A$ multiplies $E$ by 4.

Answer: D. The period is unchanged and the total energy is four times larger.
