F=ma · Dynamics and Friction — practice problem 7

F=ma · Dynamics and Friction · Step 5

A 2 kg block sits on a horizontal table and is connected by a light string over a light, frictionless pulley at the table edge to a 3 kg block hanging freely. The coefficient of kinetic friction between the 2 kg block and the table is 0.25. Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$. What is the string tension while the blocks move?

  1. 15 N
  2. 30 N
  3. 12 N
  4. 20 N
  5. 18 N
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Write Newton's second law separately for each block; they share the same acceleration and tension.

Hint 2. Friction on the table block is $\mu m g$.

Hint 3. $30-T=3a$ and $T-5=2a$.

Solution

Friction is $0.25\cdot2\cdot10=5$ N. Adding $30-T=3a$ and $T-5=2a$ gives $25=5a$, so $a=5$ m/s$^2$ and $T=30-15=15$ N. (12 N would be the frictionless result.)

Answer: A. 15 N
