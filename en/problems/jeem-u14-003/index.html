A step-down transformer draws $1000$ W from the mains and is $90\%$ efficient. Its secondary supplies a load at $30$ V. The current in the secondary is:

  1. 33.3 A
  2. 27 A
  3. 3 A
  4. 30 A
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Output power is efficiency times input power.

Hint 2. $P_{out}=0.9\times1000=900$ W.

Hint 3. $I_s=P_{out}/V_s$.

Solution

$P_{out}=0.9\times1000=900$ W. $I_s=P_{out}/V_s=900/30=30$ A.

Answer: D. 30 A
