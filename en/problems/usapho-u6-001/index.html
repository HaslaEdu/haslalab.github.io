USAPhO · Modern Physics · Step 6

A positive pion ($m_\pi c^2=139.57$ MeV) at rest decays into a positive muon ($m_\mu c^2=105.66$ MeV) and a neutrino, which you may treat as massless. Find the kinetic energy of the muon, in MeV.

Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. The muon and neutrino have equal and opposite momenta $p$; for the neutrino, $E_\nu=pc$.

Hint 2. Energy: $m_\pi c^2=\sqrt{(pc)^2+(m_\mu c^2)^2}+pc$.

Hint 3. Solve: $E_\mu=\frac{m_\pi^2+m_\mu^2}{2m_\pi}c^2$, then $K=E_\mu-m_\mu c^2=\frac{(m_\pi-m_\mu)^2}{2m_\pi}c^2$.

Solution

Momentum conservation gives equal $p$ for both particles, with $E_\nu=pc=m_\pi c^2-E_\mu$. Squaring $E_\mu^2=(pc)^2+m_\mu^2c^4$ gives $E_\mu=\frac{m_\pi^2+m_\mu^2}{2m_\pi}c^2$, so $K_\mu=\frac{(m_\pi-m_\mu)^2}{2m_\pi}c^2=\frac{33.91^2}{279.14}\approx4.12$ MeV. The neutrino carries off the remaining $\approx29.8$ MeV.

Answer: 4.12 MeV
