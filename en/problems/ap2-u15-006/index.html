AP Physics 2 · Modern Physics · Step 3

A nucleus of uranium-238, ${}^{238}_{92}\text{U}$, undergoes alpha decay. Which nucleus is produced?

  1. ${}^{234}_{92}\text{U}$
  2. ${}^{236}_{90}\text{Th}$
  3. ${}^{234}_{90}\text{Th}$
  4. ${}^{238}_{90}\text{Th}$
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. An alpha particle is a helium nucleus; what are its mass number and charge?

Hint 2. Conserve both the mass number $A$ and atomic number $Z$.

Hint 3. The daughter has $A = 238 - 4$ and $Z = 92 - 2$.

Solution

An alpha particle is ${}^{4}_{2}\text{He}$. Conservation of nucleon number gives $A = 238 - 4 = 234$ and conservation of charge gives $Z = 92 - 2 = 90$. Element 90 is thorium, so the daughter nucleus is ${}^{234}_{90}\text{Th}$.

Answer: C. ${}^{234}_{90}\text{Th}$
