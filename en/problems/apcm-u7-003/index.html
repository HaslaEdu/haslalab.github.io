AP Physics C: Mechanics · Oscillations · Step 5

A 0.40 kg mass on a spring of constant 50 N/m oscillates with amplitude 0.12 m. What is the mass's speed when it is 0.060 m from equilibrium? Answer in m/s.

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Use conservation of energy for the spring and mass.

Hint 2. $\tfrac12kA^2=\tfrac12mv^2+\tfrac12kx^2$.

Hint 3. $v=\sqrt{\dfrac km\,(A^2-x^2)}$.

Solution

$\tfrac12kA^2=\tfrac12mv^2+\tfrac12kx^2$ gives $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{m}(A^2-x^2)}=\sqrt{125(0.0144-0.0036)}=\sqrt{1.35}\approx1.16$ m/s. The final answer is about 1.16 m/s.

Answer: 1.1619 m/s
