AP Physics 1 · Oscillations · Step 4

A block oscillates horizontally on an ideal spring. At the instant the block is at its maximum displacement from equilibrium, which statement is correct?

  1. The block's speed is at its maximum and its acceleration is zero.
  2. The block's kinetic energy and spring potential energy are both zero.
  3. The block's kinetic energy is at its maximum and the spring energy is zero.
  4. The block's speed is zero and the magnitude of its acceleration is at its maximum.
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. At the turning point the block momentarily stops.

Hint 2. The restoring force is proportional to displacement.

Hint 3. Total energy there is all spring potential energy, and $a=-kx/m$ is largest.

Solution

At maximum displacement (the turning point) the block is momentarily at rest, so its kinetic energy is zero and all the energy is stored in the spring. The restoring force $F=-kx$ is largest there, so the acceleration magnitude is also largest. The two energies cannot both be zero because the total is conserved.

Answer: D. The block's speed is zero and the magnitude of its acceleration is at its maximum.
