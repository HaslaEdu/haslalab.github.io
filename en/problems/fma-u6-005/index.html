F=ma · Gravitation and Orbits · Step 6

Two identical stars, each of mass $M$, move in circular orbits about their common center, separated by a distance $d$. A tiny planet orbits a single star of mass $M$ in a circle of radius $d$. What is the ratio (period of the binary)/(period of the planet)?

  1. $1/2$
  2. $1/\sqrt2$
  3. $\sqrt2$
  4. $1$
  5. $2$
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Each star is pulled by the other, at separation $d$, but moves on a circle of smaller radius.

Hint 2. Each star's orbit radius is $d/2$.

Hint 3. $M\,(4\pi^2/T^2)(d/2)=GM^2/d^2$.

Solution

For a star: $M\omega^2(d/2)=GM^2/d^2$, so $\omega^2=2GM/d^3$ and $T_b=2\pi\sqrt{d^3/2GM}$. The planet has $T_p=2\pi\sqrt{d^3/GM}$. So $T_b/T_p=1/\sqrt2$.

Answer: B. $1/\sqrt2$
