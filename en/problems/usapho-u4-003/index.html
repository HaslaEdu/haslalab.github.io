USAPhO · Electricity and Magnetism — practice problem 18

USAPhO · Electricity and Magnetism · Step 7

Two large vertical parallel metal plates, $2.0$ mm apart, dip into a wide open dish of insulating oil (dielectric constant $2.5$, density $900$ kg/m$^3$). A battery keeps the potential difference between the plates at $5.0$ kV. The oil between the plates rises above the level of the oil in the dish. Neglecting surface tension, find the height of the rise, in mm.

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. At constant voltage the upward force on the dielectric is $F=+dU/dh$, where $U=\tfrac12CV^2$ is the capacitor energy (not the total energy including the battery).

Hint 2. With plate width $w$ and liquid height $h$, $C=\frac{\varepsilon_0w}{d}[\ell+(\kappa-1)h]$, so $F=\frac{\varepsilon_0(\kappa-1)wV^2}{2d}$.

Hint 3. Balance with the weight of the raised column $\rho g\,h\,w\,d$.

Solution

At fixed $V$, the upward force is $F=\tfrac12V^2\,dC/dh=\frac{\varepsilon_0(\kappa-1)wV^2}{2d}$, independent of $h$. The raised oil weighs $\rho g hwd$, so $h=\frac{\varepsilon_0(\kappa-1)V^2}{2\rho gd^2}=\frac{8.854\times10^{-12}(1.5)(2.5\times10^7)}{2(900)(9.8)(4\times10^{-6})}\approx4.71$ mm.

Answer: 4.706 mm
