USAPhO · Thermodynamics and Statistical Physics — practice problem 14

USAPhO · Thermodynamics and Statistical Physics · Step 6

A vertical cylinder of cross-sectional area $10.0$ cm$^2$ is closed at the bottom and contains air ($\gamma=1.40$) under a frictionless, airtight piston of mass $2.0$ kg. The outside air pressure is $1.013\times10^5$ Pa, and at equilibrium the piston is $20.0$ cm above the bottom. The piston is pushed down slightly and released. The oscillations are fast enough to be adiabatic. Find their frequency, in Hz.

Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. First find the equilibrium gas pressure: the atmosphere plus the piston's weight per unit area.

Hint 2. Adiabatic: $PV^\gamma$ is constant, so a small displacement $y$ changes the pressure by $\delta P=-\gamma P\,y/h$.

Hint 3. Restoring force $-\gamma PA\,y/h$, so $\omega^2=\gamma PA/(mh)$.

Solution

At equilibrium $P=P_0+mg/A=1.013\times10^5+19600\approx1.209\times10^5$ Pa. For a small displacement $y$, $PV^\gamma=$ const gives $\delta P=-\gamma Py/h$, so the force is $-\frac{\gamma PA}{h}y$ and $\omega^2=\frac{\gamma PA}{mh}=\frac{1.4(1.209\times10^5)(10^{-3})}{2.0\times0.20}$. So $\omega\approx20.57$ rad/s and $f=\omega/2\pi\approx3.27$ Hz.

Answer: 3.274 Hz
