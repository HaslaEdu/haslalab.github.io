JEE Main · Units and Measurements · Step 5

The dimensional formula of the quantity $\dfrac{B^{2}}{\mu_0}$, where $B$ is magnetic field and $\mu_0$ the permeability of free space, is the same as that of:

  1. $[ML^{2}T^{-2}]$
  2. $[MLT^{-2}]$
  3. $[ML^{-1}T^{-2}]$
  4. $[ML^{-2}T^{-2}]$
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Ask what physical quantity has the units of energy per unit volume.

Hint 2. $B^2/(2\mu_0)$ is the magnetic energy density.

Hint 3. Energy density has dimensions of energy divided by volume: $[ML^2T^{-2}]/[L^3]$.

Solution

$B^2/(2\mu_0)$ is the magnetic energy stored per unit volume, so $B^2/\mu_0$ has the same dimensions as energy density (or pressure). Dimensions: $[ML^2T^{-2}]/[L^3]=[ML^{-1}T^{-2}]$.

Answer: C. $[ML^{-1}T^{-2}]$
