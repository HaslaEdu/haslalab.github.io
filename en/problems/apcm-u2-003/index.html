AP Physics C: Mechanics · Force and Translational Dynamics · Step 5

An object of mass $m$ is released from rest in a fluid that exerts a drag force $-b\mathbf v$, in addition to gravity. Let $v_t=mg/b$ be its terminal speed. What is its speed at time $t=m/b$ after release?

  1. $v_t\,e^{-1}$
  2. $v_t\,(1-e^{-2})$
  3. $v_t\,(1-e)$
  4. $v_t\,(1-e^{-1})$
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Write $m\,dv/dt=mg-bv$ and solve this first-order equation with $v(0)=0$.

Hint 2. Separate variables: $dv/(v_t-v)=(b/m)\,dt$.

Hint 3. $v(t)=v_t\,(1-e^{-bt/m})$; put in $t=m/b$.

Solution

$m\,\dfrac{dv}{dt}=mg-bv$ gives $v(t)=\dfrac{mg}{b}\left(1-e^{-bt/m}\right)=v_t(1-e^{-bt/m})$. At $t=m/b$ the exponent is $-1$, so $v=v_t(1-1/e)\approx0.63\,v_t$. The correct choice is D.

Answer: D. $v_t\,(1-e^{-1})$
