A projectile is fired from the foot of a smooth incline that rises at $30^\circ$ above the horizontal. Its launch speed is 20 m/s at $60^\circ$ above the horizontal, in the vertical plane containing the line of steepest slope. Using $g=9.8$ m/s$^2$ and no air resistance, find how far up the incline (measured along the slope) it lands. Answer in meters.

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Choose axes along and perpendicular to the incline and resolve both $g$ and the launch velocity.

Hint 2. Perpendicular to the slope: $a_\perp=-g\cos30^\circ$, launch component $v\sin30^\circ$. Along the slope: $a_\parallel=-g\sin30^\circ$, launch component $v\cos30^\circ$.

Hint 3. Flight time $t=2v\sin30^\circ/(g\cos30^\circ)$, then $R=v\cos30^\circ\,t-\tfrac12 g\sin30^\circ\,t^2$.

Solution

Relative to the slope the launch angle is $30^\circ$. Perpendicular motion gives $t=\dfrac{2v\sin30^\circ}{g\cos30^\circ}=2.36$ s. Along the slope, $R=v\cos30^\circ\,t-\tfrac12 g\sin30^\circ\,t^2\approx27.2$ m.

Answer: 27.2109 m
