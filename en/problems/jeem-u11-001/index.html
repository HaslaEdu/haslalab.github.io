JEE Main · Electrostatics · Step 5

Two point charges $+q$ and $+4q$ are fixed a distance $d$ apart. The point on the line joining them where the net electric field is zero lies at a distance from the charge $+q$ of:

  1. $d/3$
  2. $d/5$
  3. $d/2$
  4. $2d/3$
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Between like charges the fields oppose; the null point is nearer the smaller charge.

Hint 2. $\dfrac{kq}{x^2}=\dfrac{4kq}{(d-x)^2}$.

Hint 3. Take the square root: $d-x=2x$.

Solution

$\dfrac{q}{x^2}=\dfrac{4q}{(d-x)^2}\Rightarrow d-x=2x\Rightarrow x=\dfrac d3$.

Answer: A. $d/3$
