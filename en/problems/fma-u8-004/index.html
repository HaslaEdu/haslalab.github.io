F=ma · Statics, Fluids and Estimation — practice problem 39

F=ma · Statics, Fluids and Estimation · Step 7

Estimate the terminal speed of a raindrop of radius 1.0 mm. Model the drag as $F=\tfrac12\rho_{\text{air}}Av^2$ with $A$ the drop's cross-sectional area, $\rho_{\text{air}}=1.2\ \text{kg/m}^3$, $\rho_{\text{water}}=1000\ \text{kg/m}^3$ and $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$. Which is closest?

  1. 47 m/s
  2. 0.47 m/s
  3. 1.5 m/s
  4. 4.7 m/s
  5. 15 m/s
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. At terminal speed, weight equals drag.

Hint 2. Write the mass using the drop's volume and the area as $\pi r^2$.

Hint 3. $\tfrac43\pi r^3\rho_wg=\tfrac12\rho_a\pi r^2v^2$.

Solution

Setting weight equal to drag: $\tfrac43\pi r^3\rho_wg=\tfrac12\rho_a\pi r^2v^2$, so $v^2=\dfrac{8\rho_w g\,r}{3\rho_a}=\dfrac{8(1000)(10)(10^{-3})}{3(1.2)}\approx22$ and $v\approx4.7$ m/s.

Answer: D. 4.7 m/s
