JEE Main · Kinetic Theory of Gases · Step 7

An ideal gas in a rigid sealed container is heated until its absolute temperature doubles. The mean collision frequency of a molecule with other molecules changes by a factor of:

  1. 2
  2. 1 (unchanged)
  3. $\sqrt2$
  4. $2\sqrt2$
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Collision frequency $=\bar v/\lambda$.

Hint 2. At fixed volume and number of molecules, $\lambda=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2\pi d^2 n}$ does not change.

Hint 3. $\bar v\propto\sqrt T$.

Solution

$\lambda\propto 1/n$ is unchanged because $n=N/V$ is fixed. The mean speed scales as $\sqrt T$, so the collision frequency $\bar v/\lambda$ increases by $\sqrt2$.

Answer: C. $\sqrt2$
