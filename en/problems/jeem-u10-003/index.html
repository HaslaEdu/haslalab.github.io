A source emits sound of frequency $640$ Hz while moving at $20$ m/s directly toward a stationary observer. The speed of sound is $340$ m/s. The observer hears a frequency of:

  1. 604 Hz
  2. 680 Hz
  3. 640 Hz
  4. 1280 Hz
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Doppler shift for a moving source: the wavelength in front is compressed.

Hint 2. $f'=f\dfrac{v}{v-v_s}$.

Hint 3. $640\times\dfrac{340}{320}$.

Solution

$f'=f\dfrac{v}{v-v_s}=640\times\dfrac{340}{320}=680$ Hz.

Answer: B. 680 Hz
