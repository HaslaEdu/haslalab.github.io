JEE Main · Magnetic Effects of Current and Magnetism · Step 5

A straight conductor $0.5$ m long carries a current of $4$ A in a uniform magnetic field of $0.5$ T. The conductor makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the field. Find the magnitude of the force on it. Answer as an integer in millinewtons (mN).

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Force on a current element is $I\,\vec L\times\vec B$.

Hint 2. $F=ILB\sin\theta$ with $\theta$ the angle between wire and field.

Hint 3. $4\times0.5\times0.5\times\sin30^\circ$ in newtons, then convert.

Solution

$F=ILB\sin\theta=4(0.5)(0.5)(0.5)=0.5$ N $=500$ mN.

Answer: 500 mN
