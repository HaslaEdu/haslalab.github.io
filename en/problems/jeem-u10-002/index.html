A stretched string of length $0.6$ m is fixed at both ends and waves travel on it at $120$ m/s. Find the frequency of its third harmonic. Answer as an integer in hertz.

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. For a string fixed at both ends, $f_n=nv/2L$.

Hint 2. The fundamental is $v/2L$.

Hint 3. Third harmonic $=3\times$ fundamental.

Solution

Fundamental $f_1=\dfrac{v}{2L}=\dfrac{120}{1.2}=100$ Hz. Third harmonic $f_3=3f_1=300$ Hz.

Answer: 300 Hz
