A ball moving at speed $v$ strikes an identical stationary ball in a perfectly elastic, glancing collision on a frictionless surface. The first ball is deflected by $30^\circ$ from its original direction. What is the speed of the second ball just after the collision?

  1. $0.50\,v$
  2. $0.87\,v$
  3. $0.25\,v$
  4. $0.71\,v$
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. For identical masses in an elastic collision with one at rest, the outgoing velocities are perpendicular.

Hint 2. So the second ball moves at $60^\circ$ on the other side; use momentum components or the right triangle of velocities.

Hint 3. $v_1'=v\cos30^\circ$, $v_2'=v\sin30^\circ$.

Solution

Momentum conservation makes $\vec v=\vec v_1'+\vec v_2'$, and energy conservation makes $v^2=v_1'^2+v_2'^2$, so the outgoing velocities are perpendicular (right triangle with hypotenuse $v$). Then $v_1'=v\cos30^\circ=0.87v$ and $v_2'=v\sin30^\circ=0.50v$. The correct choice is A.

Answer: A. $0.50\,v$
