AP Physics C: Mechanics · Force and Translational Dynamics · Step 7

A uniform flexible rope of length 2.0 m lies on a frictionless horizontal table with one quarter of its length hanging over the edge, then is released from rest. The rope stays in contact with the edge without friction. Using $g=9.8$ m/s$^2$, find the speed of the rope at the instant its last end leaves the table. Answer in m/s.

Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Only the hanging part is pulled by gravity, but the whole rope accelerates.

Hint 2. If a length $x$ hangs, $M\ddot x=(M x/L)\,g$, so $\ddot x=gx/L$.

Hint 3. Use $\ddot x=v\,dv/dx$ and integrate from $x=L/4$ to $x=L$: $v^2=\dfrac gL\left(L^2-x_0^2\right)$.

Solution

With $x$ hanging, $\ddot x=\dfrac{g}{L}x$. Then $v\,dv=\dfrac gL x\,dx$ gives $\tfrac12v^2=\dfrac{g}{2L}(L^2-x_0^2)$ with $x_0=L/4$. So $v^2=gL\left(1-\tfrac1{16}\right)=9.8(2.0)(0.9375)=18.4$ and $v\approx4.29$ m/s.

Answer: 4.2866 m/s
