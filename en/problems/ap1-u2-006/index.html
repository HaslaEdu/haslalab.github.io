AP Physics 1 · Force and Translational Dynamics · Step 6

A 5.0 kg block is pulled up a $37^\circ$ incline by a force parallel to the incline. The coefficient of kinetic friction is 0.30 and the block accelerates up the incline at 1.5 m/s$^2$. Use $g=9.8$ m/s$^2$. What is the magnitude of the pulling force? Answer in newtons.

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Choose axes along and perpendicular to the incline.

Hint 2. Friction points down the incline because the block moves up.

Hint 3. $F-mg\sin\theta-\mu_k mg\cos\theta=ma$.

Solution

Perpendicular: $N=mg\cos37^\circ=39.13$ N, so friction is $0.30N=11.74$ N. Gravity along the slope is $mg\sin37^\circ=29.49$ N. Along the incline $F=ma+mg\sin\theta+f_k=7.5+29.49+11.74=48.7$ N.

Answer: 48.73 N
