USAPhO · Rotation and Oscillations · Step 6

A bowling ball (treat it as a uniform solid sphere) is launched along a lane with speed $8.0$ m/s and no initial spin. The coefficient of kinetic friction between ball and lane is $0.10$. How far does the ball travel before it begins to roll without slipping? Give the answer in m.

Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Friction slows the translation and at the same time spins the ball up. Rolling begins when $v=\omega R$.

Hint 2. $v=v_0-\mu gt$ and $\omega=\frac{\mu mgR}{\frac25mR^2}t=\frac{5\mu g}{2R}t$.

Hint 3. Rolling starts at $t=\frac{2v_0}{7\mu g}$; then $d=v_0t-\tfrac12\mu gt^2$.

Solution

Friction $\mu mg$ decelerates the centre, $v=v_0-\mu gt$, and its torque spins the ball up, $\omega R=\tfrac52\mu gt$. These are equal at $t=2v_0/(7\mu g)$, so $d=v_0t-\tfrac12\mu gt^2=\frac{12v_0^2}{49\mu g}=\frac{12(64)}{49(0.98)}\approx16.0$ m. The final speed is $5v_0/7$.

Answer: 15.99 m
