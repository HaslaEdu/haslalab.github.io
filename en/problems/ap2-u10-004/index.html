AP Physics 2 · Electric Force, Field, and Potential · Step 4

A uniform electric field points in the $+x$ direction. Point A is at $x = 1\ \text{m}$ and point B is at $x = 3\ \text{m}$. Which statement is correct?

  1. The electric potential at B is higher than at A, and a negative charge moved from A to B gains electric potential energy.
  2. The electric potential at A is higher than at B, and a negative charge moved from A to B gains electric potential energy.
  3. The electric potential at A is higher than at B, and a negative charge moved from A to B loses electric potential energy.
  4. The potentials at A and B are equal because the field is uniform.
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Does potential increase or decrease along the direction of the field?

Hint 2. Positive charges lose potential energy moving along the field; negative charges do the opposite.

Hint 3. Use $U = qV$ with $q < 0$ and $V_B < V_A$.

Solution

Potential decreases in the direction of the electric field, so $V_A > V_B$. For a charge $q$, $\Delta U = q\Delta V$. Moving from A to B, $\Delta V < 0$, and with $q < 0$ this gives $\Delta U > 0$, so the negative charge gains potential energy (the field does negative work on it).

Answer: B. The electric potential at A is higher than at B, and a negative charge moved from A to B gains electric potential energy.
