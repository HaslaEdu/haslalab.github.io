AP Physics C: Mechanics · Energy and Momentum of Rotating Systems · Step 6

A thin disk of mass $M$ and radius $R$ has surface mass density $\sigma(r)=\sigma_0\,r/R$, increasing toward the rim. What is its moment of inertia about the axis through its center, perpendicular to the disk?

  1. $\dfrac12MR^2$
  2. $\dfrac25MR^2$
  3. $\dfrac35MR^2$
  4. $\dfrac23MR^2$
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Use thin rings: $dm=\sigma\,2\pi r\,dr$ and $dI=r^2\,dm$. Find $M$ in terms of $\sigma_0$ first.

Hint 2. $M=\int_0^R\sigma_0\frac rR\,2\pi r\,dr=\tfrac23\pi\sigma_0R^2$.

Hint 3. $I=\int_0^R\frac{2\pi\sigma_0}{R}r^4\,dr=\tfrac25\pi\sigma_0R^4$.

Solution

$M=\dfrac{2\pi\sigma_0}{R}\displaystyle\int_0^Rr^2dr=\dfrac{2\pi\sigma_0R^2}{3}$. $I=\dfrac{2\pi\sigma_0}{R}\displaystyle\int_0^Rr^4dr=\dfrac{2\pi\sigma_0R^4}{5}$. Dividing, $I/M=\dfrac{3}{5}R^2$. It is larger than $\tfrac12MR^2$ (uniform disk) since mass is shifted outward.

Answer: C. $\dfrac35MR^2$
