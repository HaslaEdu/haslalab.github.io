F=ma · Rotation and Angular Momentum · Step 7

A solid uniform sphere slides at 7.0 m/s along a smooth floor with no spin, then enters a rough region where friction eventually makes it roll without slipping. What is its center-of-mass speed once it rolls without slipping?

  1. 3.5 m/s
  2. 6.0 m/s
  3. 4.7 m/s
  4. 5.0 m/s
  5. 7.0 m/s
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Which point on the sphere is a good axis, so that friction exerts no torque about it?

Hint 2. Conserve angular momentum about a point on the floor under the center's line of motion.

Hint 3. $mv_0R=(mR^2+\tfrac25mR^2)\,\omega$, and $v=\omega R$.

Solution

Friction acts at the contact point, so angular momentum about the floor is conserved: $mv_0R=mvR+\tfrac25mR^2(v/R)$. Hence $v=v_0/(1+2/5)=\tfrac57v_0=5.0$ m/s. (4.7 m/s is the result for a solid cylinder, $\tfrac23v_0$.)

Answer: D. 5.0 m/s
