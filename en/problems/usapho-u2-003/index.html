USAPhO · Rotation and Oscillations · Step 6

A uniform thin rod of length $1.2$ m is drilled with a small hole at an adjustable distance from its centre and hung on a horizontal frictionless nail through the hole, so that it swings in a vertical plane as a physical pendulum. The hole position is chosen to make the period of small oscillations as SHORT as possible. What is this minimum period, in s?

Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. For pivot distance $x$ from the centre, $T=2\pi\sqrt{I_p/(mgx)}$ with $I_p=mL^2/12+mx^2$.

Hint 2. Minimize $f(x)=\frac{L^2/12+x^2}{x}$; it diverges both as $x\to0$ and as $x\to\infty$.

Hint 3. The minimum is at $x=L/\sqrt{12}$, where $f=2x=L/\sqrt3$.

Solution

$T(x)=2\pi\sqrt{(L^2/12+x^2)/(gx)}$. Minimizing $L^2/(12x)+x$ gives $x=L/\sqrt{12}\approx0.346$ m, where the bracket equals $2x=L/\sqrt3$. Then $T_{min}=2\pi\sqrt{L/(\sqrt3g)}=2\pi\sqrt{1.2/(1.732\times9.8)}\approx1.67$ s.

Answer: 1.671 s
