AP Physics 2 · Geometric Optics · Step 4

Light travels in glass (index 1.50) toward a flat boundary with water (index 1.33). For which of the following angles of incidence, measured in the glass from the normal, will the light undergo total internal reflection at the boundary?

  1. 30°
  2. 65°
  3. 45°
  4. 58°
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Total internal reflection needs light going from higher to lower index; is that the case here?

Hint 2. Find the critical angle where the refraction angle would be 90°.

Hint 3. $\sin\theta_c = n_2/n_1 = 1.33/1.50$.

Solution

The glass-to-water direction is from higher to lower index, so total internal reflection is possible. The critical angle satisfies $\sin\theta_c = 1.33/1.50 = 0.887$, so $\theta_c = 62.5°$. Only angles of incidence larger than this give total internal reflection, so only 65° qualifies; 30°, 45° and 58° are all below $\theta_c$.

Answer: B. 65°
