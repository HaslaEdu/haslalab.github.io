AP Physics C: Mechanics · Linear Momentum · Step 4

An 8.0 kg cart moving at 1.5 m/s along a track is pushed in the direction of motion by a force $F(t)=24t-6t^2$ (newtons) for $0\le t\le4.0$ s. What is its speed after the push?

  1. $9.5$ m/s
  2. $8.0$ m/s
  3. $13.5$ m/s
  4. $6.5$ m/s
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Use the impulse-momentum theorem with an integral of the force.

Hint 2. $J=\int_0^4F\,dt$ and $J=m\,\Delta v$.

Hint 3. $J=[12t^2-2t^3]_0^4=64$ N s.

Solution

$J=\int_0^4(24t-6t^2)\,dt=[12t^2-2t^3]_0^4=192-128=64$ N s. Then $\Delta v=J/m=8.0$ m/s and $v=1.5+8.0=9.5$ m/s. Forgetting the initial speed gives 8.0.

Answer: A. $9.5$ m/s
