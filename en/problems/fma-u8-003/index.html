A tall tank is kept filled with water to a depth of 1.0 m above the ground. A small hole is made in its side at a height of 0.40 m above the ground. Ignoring viscosity, how far from the base of the tank (horizontally) does the jet hit the ground?

  1. 1.2 m
  2. 0.80 m
  3. 0.69 m
  4. 0.98 m
  5. 0.49 m
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Find the exit speed from Bernoulli's equation (Torricelli's result).

Hint 2. Then treat the jet as a projectile launched horizontally from 0.40 m.

Hint 3. $v=\sqrt{2g\,d}$ with $d=0.60$ m, and $t=\sqrt{2(0.40)/g}$.

Solution

The hole is 0.60 m below the surface, so $v=\sqrt{2g(0.6)}$. The fall time from 0.40 m is $t=\sqrt{2(0.4)/g}$. Range $=vt=2\sqrt{0.6\cdot0.4}\approx0.98$ m. (The $g$ cancels, so the result doesn't depend on its value.)

Answer: D. 0.98 m
