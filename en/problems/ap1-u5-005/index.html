AP Physics 1 · Torque and Rotational Dynamics · Step 5

A wheel is spinning up under a net torque $\tau_0$ with angular acceleration $\alpha_0$. The net torque is doubled while a ring of material is added that triples the wheel's rotational inertia. What is the new angular acceleration?

  1. $\tfrac{2}{3}\alpha_0$
  2. $\tfrac{3}{2}\alpha_0$
  3. $\tfrac{1}{6}\alpha_0$
  4. $6\alpha_0$
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Use $\alpha=\tau/I$.

Hint 2. Torque is in the numerator and rotational inertia in the denominator.

Hint 3. New $\alpha=(2\tau_0)/(3I_0)$.

Solution

Since $\alpha=\tau_{net}/I$, the new value is $(2\tau_0)/(3I_0)=\tfrac23\alpha_0$. Torque raises $\alpha$ and rotational inertia lowers it.

Answer: A. $\tfrac{2}{3}\alpha_0$
