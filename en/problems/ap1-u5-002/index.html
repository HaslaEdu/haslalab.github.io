AP Physics 1 · Torque and Rotational Dynamics · Step 3

A uniform seesaw is balanced on a pivot at its center. A 40 kg child sits 1.5 m from the pivot. Where must a 30 kg child sit on the other side to balance it?

  1. 1.1 m from the pivot
  2. 1.5 m from the pivot
  3. 2.0 m from the pivot
  4. 2.7 m from the pivot
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Balance means the net torque about the pivot is zero.

Hint 2. Each child's torque is weight times distance.

Hint 3. $m_1 d_1=m_2 d_2$.

Solution

For rotational equilibrium the torques about the pivot cancel: $m_1 g d_1=m_2 g d_2$. So $d_2=m_1d_1/m_2=(40)(1.5)/30=2.0$ m. The lighter child must sit farther from the pivot.

Answer: C. 2.0 m from the pivot
