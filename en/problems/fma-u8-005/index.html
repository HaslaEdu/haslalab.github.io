A uniform horizontal beam of weight 200 N and length 4.0 m rests on two supports: one at its left end and one 3.0 m from the left end (1.0 m from the right end). A load is hung from the right end. What is the largest load for which the beam does not tip up off the left support?

  1. 100 N
  2. 300 N
  3. 400 N
  4. 200 N
  5. 150 N
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. At the point of tipping, the left support's force is zero.

Hint 2. Take torques about the right-hand support.

Hint 3. Beam's weight acts 1.0 m to the left of the right support, the load 1.0 m to the right.

Solution

When the beam is about to lift off the left support, the only forces are the support at 3.0 m, the beam's weight (at 2.0 m) and the load $w$ (at 4.0 m). Torques about the support: $200(1.0)=w(1.0)$, so $w=200$ N.

Answer: D. 200 N
