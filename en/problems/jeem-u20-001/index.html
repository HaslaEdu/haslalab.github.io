JEE Main · Experimental Skills · Step 5

A vernier caliper has $10$ vernier divisions equal to $9$ main scale divisions, with $1$ main scale division $=1$ mm. When the jaws are closed the zero of the vernier is $0.3$ mm to the right of the main-scale zero (the $3$rd vernier line coincides). Measuring a rod, the main scale reading is $24$ mm and the $6$th vernier division coincides. The corrected length is:

  1. 24.9 mm
  2. 24.6 mm
  3. 24.3 mm
  4. 23.7 mm
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Least count $=1$ MSD $-1$ VSD $=0.1$ mm.

Hint 2. Observed reading $=24+6\times0.1$.

Hint 3. Subtract the positive zero error.

Solution

Least count $=1-0.9=0.1$ mm. Observed $=24+0.6=24.6$ mm. Zero error is $+0.3$ mm, so corrected $=24.6-0.3=24.3$ mm.

Answer: C. 24.3 mm
