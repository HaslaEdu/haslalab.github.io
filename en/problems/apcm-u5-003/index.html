AP Physics C: Mechanics · Torque and Rotational Dynamics · Step 6

A uniform solid cylinder rolls without slipping down a rough incline of angle $\theta$. What is the minimum coefficient of static friction needed to prevent slipping?

  1. $\tan\theta$
  2. $\tfrac13\tan\theta$
  3. $\tfrac23\tan\theta$
  4. $\tfrac12\tan\theta$
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Friction provides the torque that makes it roll, so find the friction force actually required.

Hint 2. Rolling down: $a=\tfrac23g\sin\theta$. Then $mg\sin\theta-f=ma$.

Hint 3. $f=\tfrac13mg\sin\theta\le\mu_s\,mg\cos\theta$.

Solution

With $I=\tfrac12mR^2$, $a=\dfrac{g\sin\theta}{1+1/2}=\tfrac23g\sin\theta$. Then $f=mg\sin\theta-ma=\tfrac13mg\sin\theta$. Rolling without slipping requires $f\le\mu_smg\cos\theta$, so $\mu_s\ge\tfrac13\tan\theta$.

Answer: B. $\tfrac13\tan\theta$
