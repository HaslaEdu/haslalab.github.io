JEE Main · Laws of Motion · Step 5

Two blocks of masses $2$ kg and $3$ kg lie in contact on a rough horizontal floor (coefficient of kinetic friction $0.1$ for both). A horizontal force of $20$ N pushes the $2$ kg block, which in turn pushes the $3$ kg block. Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$. Find the contact force between the blocks. Answer as an integer in newtons.

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Treat both blocks as one system to find the common acceleration.

Hint 2. $a=\dfrac{F-\mu(m_1+m_2)g}{m_1+m_2}$.

Hint 3. The contact force is the net push needed on the $3$ kg block: $N=m_2a+\mu m_2g$.

Solution

$a=\dfrac{20-0.1(5)(10)}{5}=3$ m/s$^2$. For the 3 kg block: $N-0.1(3)(10)=3(3)$, so $N=9+3=12$ N.

Answer: 12 N
