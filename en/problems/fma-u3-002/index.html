F=ma · Energy and Work — practice problem 12

F=ma · Energy and Work · Step 5

A 1000 kg car's engine delivers a constant 40 kW to the driving wheels. The total resistive force opposing the motion is a constant 1000 N. What is the car's acceleration when its speed is 10 m/s?

  1. 3.0 m/s$^2$
  2. 1.0 m/s$^2$
  3. 2.0 m/s$^2$
  4. 4.0 m/s$^2$
  5. 0.4 m/s$^2$
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Power and speed together fix the driving force.

Hint 2. The net force is the driving force minus the resistance.

Hint 3. $F=P/v$, then $a=(F-f)/m$.

Solution

Driving force $F=P/v=40000/10=4000$ N. Net force $=4000-1000=3000$ N, so $a=3.0$ m/s$^2$. (4.0 m/s$^2$ forgets the resistance.)

Answer: A. 3.0 m/s$^2$
