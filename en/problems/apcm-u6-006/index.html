A solid sphere and a thin hoop of the same mass and radius are released from rest at the same height on a ramp and roll without slipping to the bottom. What is the ratio of the sphere's speed to the hoop's speed at the bottom?

  1. $1.43$
  2. $1.20$
  3. $0.84$
  4. $1.41$
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Use energy conservation with the proper $I=kmR^2$ for each shape.

Hint 2. $v^2=\dfrac{2gh}{1+k}$, with $k=2/5$ for the sphere and $k=1$ for the hoop.

Hint 3. Ratio $=\sqrt{\dfrac{1+1}{1+2/5}}$.

Solution

$v=\sqrt{\dfrac{2gh}{1+k}}$. Sphere: $v^2=\dfrac{10gh}{7}$; hoop: $v^2=gh$. The ratio of speeds is $\sqrt{10/7}\approx1.20$. The value 1.43 is the ratio of squares.

Answer: B. $1.20$
