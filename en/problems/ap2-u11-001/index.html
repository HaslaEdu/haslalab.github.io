AP Physics 2 · Electric Circuits · Step 3

An ideal 12 V battery is connected to a 4.0 Ω resistor in series with a parallel pair of resistors. The parallel pair consists of a 6.0 Ω resistor and a 12 Ω resistor. What is the current supplied by the battery, in amperes?

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. First reduce the parallel pair to one equivalent resistor.

Hint 2. $1/R_p = 1/R_1 + 1/R_2$; then add the series resistor.

Hint 3. $I = \varepsilon / R_{eq}$.

Solution

The parallel pair has $1/R_p = 1/6.0 + 1/12 = 1/4.0$, so $R_p = 4.0\ \Omega$. In series with 4.0 Ω this gives $R_{eq} = 8.0\ \Omega$. The battery current is $I = 12/8.0 = 1.50\ \text{A}$.

Answer: 1.5 A
