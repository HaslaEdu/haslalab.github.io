AP Physics 2 · Electric Force, Field, and Potential · Step 4

A point charge $+2.0$ nC and a point charge $-2.0$ nC are fixed 0.40 m apart on a horizontal line. Using $k = 8.99\times10^{9}\ \text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$, find the magnitude of the net electric field at the midpoint between them, in N/C.

Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. Draw the field direction from each charge at the midpoint.

Hint 2. Do the two fields point the same way or oppose each other?

Hint 3. Both point toward the negative charge, so add $kq/r^2$ twice with $r = 0.20$ m.

Solution

At the midpoint, the field from $+2.0$ nC points away from it and the field from $-2.0$ nC points toward it; both point along the line toward the negative charge, so they add. Each has magnitude $kq/r^2 = (8.99\times10^9)(2.0\times10^{-9})/(0.20)^2 = 449.5\ \text{N/C}$. The net field is $2\times449.5 = 899\ \text{N/C}$.

Answer: 899 N/C
