USAPhO · Rotation and Oscillations — practice problem 9

USAPhO · Rotation and Oscillations · Step 7

Two gliders, each of mass $0.20$ kg, sit on a horizontal frictionless air track. Each is attached to its own end wall by a spring of constant $20$ N/m, and the two gliders are joined to each other by a weak spring of constant $1.0$ N/m. All springs are relaxed at equilibrium. Glider A is pulled aside and released from rest while glider B is held at equilibrium and released at the same instant. Glider A's oscillation amplitude slowly dies away while B's grows. After how long does the amplitude of A first fall to zero, with essentially all the energy in B? Give the answer in s.

Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Find the two normal modes: the gliders moving together, and the gliders moving oppositely.

Hint 2. $\omega_1=\sqrt{k/m}$ (in phase, coupling spring unstretched), $\omega_2=\sqrt{(k+2k_c)/m}$ (out of phase).

Hint 3. $x_A\propto\cos\frac{\omega_2-\omega_1}{2}t\,\cos\frac{\omega_1+\omega_2}{2}t$; the envelope vanishes first when $\frac{\omega_2-\omega_1}{2}t=\frac\pi2$.

Solution

The initial condition is an equal mix of the two modes, $x_A=\tfrac{x_0}2(\cos\omega_1t+\cos\omega_2t)=x_0\cos\frac{\Delta\omega}2t\cos\bar\omega t$, with $\omega_1=\sqrt{k/m}=10.00$ rad/s and $\omega_2=\sqrt{(k+2k_c)/m}\approx10.488$ rad/s. Glider A's envelope first vanishes at $t=\pi/(\omega_2-\omega_1)\approx6.4$ s, when all the energy is in B.

Answer: 6.437 s
