A Carnot engine has an efficiency of $40\%$. When the temperature of its sink is lowered by $50$ K (source unchanged), the efficiency becomes $50\%$. Find the source temperature. Answer as an integer in kelvin.

Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Efficiency of a Carnot engine is $1-T_2/T_1$.

Hint 2. Write the two efficiency equations and subtract.

Hint 3. $\dfrac{50}{T_1}=0.5-0.4$.

Solution

$1-T_2/T_1=0.4$ and $1-(T_2-50)/T_1=0.5$. Subtracting: $50/T_1=0.1$, so $T_1=500$ K.

Answer: 500 K
