F=ma · Energy and Work — practice problem 11

F=ma · Energy and Work · Step 5

A 0.50 kg block compresses a spring ($k=200$ N/m) by 0.30 m on a horizontal table and is released. After leaving the spring it crosses a rough 1.0 m stretch (kinetic friction coefficient 0.40), then slides up a frictionless ramp. Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$. What maximum height above the table does it reach?

  1. 1.8 m
  2. 2.0 m
  3. 1.0 m
  4. 0.90 m
  5. 1.4 m
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Use energy: where does the stored spring energy go?

Hint 2. Friction removes $\mu m g d$ of energy on the rough stretch.

Hint 3. $\tfrac12 kx^2-\mu m g d=mgh$.

Solution

Spring energy $=\tfrac12(200)(0.3)^2=9$ J. Friction removes $0.4\cdot0.5\cdot10\cdot1.0=2$ J. The remaining 7 J becomes $mgh=5h$, so $h=1.4$ m. (1.8 m ignores friction.)

Answer: E. 1.4 m
