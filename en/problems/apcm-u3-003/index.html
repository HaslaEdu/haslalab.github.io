AP Physics C: Mechanics · Work, Energy, and Power · Step 5

A 1200 kg car's engine delivers a constant mechanical power of 60 kW to the wheels. Ignoring air drag and rolling resistance, what is the car's acceleration at the instant its speed is 20 m/s on a level road?

  1. $5.0$ m/s$^2$
  2. $1.25$ m/s$^2$
  3. $2.5$ m/s$^2$
  4. $50$ m/s$^2$
Solve it on HaslaLab and have your answer checked →
Hints

Hint 1. Power relates force to speed.

Hint 2. Use $P=Fv$ and then Newton's second law.

Hint 3. $a=P/(mv)$.

Solution

$P=Fv$ so $F=P/v=60000/20=3000$ N and $a=F/m=3000/1200=2.5$ m/s$^2$. The acceleration falls as speed rises. The correct choice is C.

Answer: C. $2.5$ m/s$^2$
