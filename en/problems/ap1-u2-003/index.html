A 60 kg person stands on a scale in an elevator that is accelerating upward at 2.0 m/s$^2$. Use $g=9.8$ m/s$^2$. What does the scale read (the normal force)?

  1. 120 N
  2. 468 N
  3. 588 N
  4. 708 N
Solve it with instant grading, hints and an AI tutor →
Hints

Hint 1. The scale reading is the normal force on the person.

Hint 2. Upward acceleration means the net force points up.

Hint 3. Apply $N-mg=ma$ with upward positive.

Solution

Taking up as positive, $N-mg=ma$, so $N=m(g+a)=60(9.8+2.0)=708$ N. This is larger than the 588 N weight because the net force must point upward.

Answer: D. 708 N
