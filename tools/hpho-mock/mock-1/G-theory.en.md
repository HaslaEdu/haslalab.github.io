Read these instructions before the exam starts. You may look at them again at any time during the exam.

## The exam

- The exam lasts 5 hours. There are 3 problems, each worth 10 points. You may do them in any order.
- Each problem has its own problem pages (Q), answer sheet (A) and writing sheets (W). The first number of a page label is the problem number: Q2-3 is the third page of problem 2.
- The constants and data you need are given in each problem.
- You may not get help from anyone, from AI, from the internet or from books during the exam. Questions about the content of the problems are not answered.

## What you may use

- Pencils, pens, an eraser, a ruler and one scientific calculator. Graphing or programmable calculators and calculator apps on a phone or computer are not allowed.
- The printed answer sheets and writing sheets, and blank A4 paper.

## Writing your answers

- Write each final answer in its box on the answer sheet. Anything outside the box is not treated as a final answer.
- Write your working on the writing sheets. If an answer on the answer sheet is wrong or missing, the graders look at the writing sheets for partial credit. Cross out with a large X anything you do not want graded.
- Use each writing sheet for one problem only, and write the question number (for example B.3) at the top of every sheet you use.
- Write on the front of each page only. Nothing on the back is graded.
- Use equations, sketches and short sentences where you can. Long explanations are not needed.
- Give numerical answers with a number of significant figures that suits the data, and with units. Use SI units unless told otherwise.
- Write only your candidate code on the answer sheets and writing sheets. Do not write your name, your school or anything else that identifies you.

## When the exam ends

- Put your pen down as soon as the end time comes. Writing after that counts as cheating.
- Photograph every answer sheet and every writing sheet you used, one page at a time, and upload them before the upload closes. Unused writing sheets need not be uploaded.
