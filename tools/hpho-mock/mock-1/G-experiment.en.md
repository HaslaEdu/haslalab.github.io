Read these instructions before the exam starts. You may look at them again at any time during the exam.

## The exam

- The exam lasts 5 hours. There are 2 problems, each worth 10 points. You may do them in any order.
- Each problem has its own problem pages (Q), answer sheet (A) and writing sheets (W). The first number of a page label is the problem number.
- In HPhO rounds the experiments are done on the HaslaLab virtual bench. This sample paper is written for real equipment; without it, practise the analysis with the sample measurements in the solution.
- You may not get help from anyone, from AI, from the internet or from books during the exam. Questions about the content of the problems are not answered.

## What you may use

- Pencils, pens, an eraser, a ruler and one scientific calculator. Graphing or programmable calculators and calculator apps on a phone or computer are not allowed.
- The printed answer sheets and writing sheets, and blank A4 paper.

## Measuring and recording

- Enter your measurements in the tables on the answer sheet, with the quantity and its unit at the head of each column.
- Write each reading with the digits you actually read, and say in one line how you chose its uncertainty.
- Draw graphs on the grids of the answer sheet. Label the axes with quantities and units, mark the scales and draw the fitted line or curve. To find a slope, use two points on the fitted line, not data points, and mark them on the graph.
- Write each final answer in its box on the answer sheet, with significant figures that match its uncertainty.

## Writing your answers

- Write your working and calculations on the writing sheets. Cross out with a large X anything you do not want graded.
- Write the question number (for example B.3) at the top of every writing sheet you use, and write on the front of each page only.
- Write only your candidate code on the answer sheets and writing sheets. Do not write your name, your school or anything else that identifies you.

## When the exam ends

- Put your pen down as soon as the end time comes. Writing after that counts as cheating.
- Photograph every answer sheet and every writing sheet you used, one page at a time, and upload them before the upload closes.
