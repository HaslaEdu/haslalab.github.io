Question 2. A physics student is investigating the conditions for static equilibrium of objects found on her study desk. First she fixes a cylindrical can of radius $r = 5.00\ \mathrm{cm}$ to a horizontal table, and on its top she rests a uniform plastic ruler of length $L = 30.0\ \mathrm{cm}$ and mass $m = 40.0\ \mathrm{g}$ in the static equilibrium situation shown in figure A. Then she applies a vertical force $\vec F$ at one of the ends of the ruler and observes that the point where the ruler rests on the cylinder shifts as shown in figure B. She observes that the ruler can take up static equilibrium configurations as long as $\theta \le 30^\circ$. Determine (a) the coefficient of static friction between the ruler and the can and (b) the magnitude of the external force in the situation where $\theta = 30^\circ$.

Question 5. A ring of radius $r$ and mass $m$ rolls without slipping on a horizontal plane, with coefficients of static and kinetic friction both equal to $\mu$, when at the instant $t = 0$ it collides with a smooth wall. Before the collision the velocity of the centre of mass of the ring is $V_0$, and the collision is instantaneous and perfectly elastic, so that its only effect is a horizontal impulse applied to the ring. Determine (a) the instant from which the velocity of the centre of the ring is constant and (b) the value of this velocity. (c) Repeat the problem with the ring replaced by a uniform disc of the same dimensions. (Data: moment of inertia of the ring $I_a = mr^2$ and of the disc $I_d = mr^2/2$.)

Question 6. An electron (charge $-e$) moves with constant speed $v_0$ in a circular orbit of radius $R$ owing to the action of a central force (for all purposes, the circular orbit can be regarded as a current loop). During the time interval $\tau$ a magnetic field appears, perpendicular to the plane of the orbit, which varies from $0$ to $B$. (a) Show that the change in the speed of the electron is independent of $\tau$. (b) If the angular momentum of the electron were quantized by the same rule adopted by Bohr for an electron in the hydrogen atom, $L = n\frac{h}{2\pi}$, where $h$ is Planck’s constant and $n$ is an integer, what would be the smallest value of $B$ capable of inducing a change in the speed of the electron?

Question 7. If a container holding a rarefied gas has a small opening, a phenomenon called effusion occurs, in which the number of molecules leaving the container is proportional to $n\overline{v}$, where $n$ is the density of the gas and $\overline{v}$ is the mean speed of the molecules. Consider a container divided into two chambers, with a small opening between them, that holds a rarefied gas. The conditions are such that effusion occurs between one chamber and the other. If chambers 1 and 2 are kept, respectively, at temperatures $T_1$ and $T_2$, and the pressure in chamber 1 is $P_1$, what is the pressure in chamber 2 at equilibrium?
