Question 1. Two charged particles, with charges $q_1 = 3\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ and $q_2 = 12\,\mu\mathrm{C}$, are held fixed a distance $d = 7.2\ \mathrm{cm}$ apart. A third particle, of charge $q = 3\,\mu\mathrm{C}$ and mass $m = 5\ \mathrm{g}$, is launched with velocity $\vec v$ from a very large distance and must reach a point located between the fixed particles. What is the smallest value of $v = |\vec v|$ compatible with this possibility?

Question 2.

A horizontal rotating disc of radius $R$ has a straight groove passing through its centre, inside which a block of mass $m$ can slide without friction. Two identical springs, of spring constant $k$ and negligible mass, are placed in the groove, each with one end attached to the rim of the disc and the other to the block. See the figure. Assume that the system is set rotating with constant angular velocity $\omega$.

(a)Determine the maximum value $\omega_m$ of the angular velocity of the disc below which the motion of the block relative to the disc can be oscillatory.

(b)For $\omega < \omega_m$, what is the oscillation frequency of the block’s motion relative to the disc?

(c)Describe the possible motions of the block relative to the disc when $\omega > \omega_m$.

Question 3. A radio station operating at a frequency of $75\ \mathrm{MHz}$ is located at the edge of the urban area of a city. The station has an antenna of power $P_0$ that sends the signal isotropically. In order to avoid wasting energy by sending the signal to the rural area to the west, the owners of the radio station decide to direct the signal to the east, where their audience lives. Knowing that this effect can be obtained by interference between signals, they decide to install a second antenna at a distance $d \ge 10\ \mathrm{m}$ to the west of the first one (the minimum distance is a technical requirement of the installation). Once installed, each antenna operates with power $P$, and the signal of the second antenna is emitted with a phase difference $\phi$ relative to the first. Assume a perfectly flat region. Determine:

(a)The ratio $P/P_0$ for which the signal in the new configuration reaches the urban area with the same intensity as before.

(b)The smallest installation distance $d$ between the antennas.

(c)The smallest value of $|\phi|$ in degrees. Should the signal of the second antenna be delayed, in phase, or advanced relative to the first?
