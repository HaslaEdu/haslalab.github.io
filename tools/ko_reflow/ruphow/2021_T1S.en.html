Preface: The last “shoot-out” between Leopold and the mice took place in 2002; since then the mice had not bothered the cat. Eighteen years later, in 2020, new underwater pebbles turned up for the legendary slingshot, and the mice decided to “relive the good old days”.

Note: no animals were harmed during the shoot-out.

Let us find the maximum possible time after which the velocities of the stone and Leopold become perpendicular again. Draw the velocity triangles of the stone and of Leopold for the closest approach, combining them into one quadrilateral. Note that this quadrilateral can be inscribed in a circle, since the sum of its opposite angles is 180°. The diameter of the circle is fixed and equal to $v_{\mathrm{rel}}$.
