A point mass $m$ is fixed at the end of a weightless rod that can rotate freely about a horizontal axis $O$. Initially the point mass is in a position of stable equilibrium under the action of a force $F$ directed perpendicular to the rod (see figure). At some moment the direction of the force $F$ is instantly reversed. After that the force also remains constant in magnitude and directed perpendicular to the rod, turning it counterclockwise.

For which values of $F$ can the rod make a full revolution about the axis of rotation?
