Express the kinetic energy $E_k$ of the system in terms of $m$ and $v$ for $m \gg \lambda R$ (here $R$ is the unknown radius of the cylinder).

Only the cylinder and the part of the tape on it have kinetic energy. This system is a uniform thin-walled cylinder of mass $m$. From König's theorem we have:

$E_k = \dfrac{mv^2}{2} + E_{k(\mathrm{rel})}.$

where $E_{k(\mathrm{rel})}$ is the kinetic energy of the system in the reference frame attached to its centre of mass.

Since the tape is inextensible, the point $O$ where its vertical part touches the cylinder is at rest. Then the angular velocity of the cylinder is $\omega = v/R$, where $R$ is the radius of the cylinder. Hence, in the centre-of-mass frame we have:

$E_{k(\mathrm{rel})} = \dfrac{m\omega^2R^2}{2} = \dfrac{mv^2}{2} \Rightarrow E_k = mv^2.$
