Part B. A capacitor with a dielectric

A parallel-plate capacitor of plate area $S$ and plate separation $d \ll \sqrt{S}$ is filled with a dielectric of dielectric constant $\varepsilon$ and resistivity $\rho$. The dielectric and the plates are in good electrical contact. Let the potential difference between the capacitor plates be $V$.

Find the charges $q$ on the metal plates of the capacitor. Express the answer in terms of $V$, $S$, $d$, $\varepsilon$ and physical constants.

Find the current $I$ flowing through the dielectric. Express the answer in terms of $V$, $S$, $d$, $\rho$ and physical constants.

It follows from the two previous parts that, in an electric circuit, such a capacitor is equivalent to some combination of a resistor of resistance $R$ and a capacitor of capacitance $C$.

Find $R$ and $C$, and state how these elements are connected (in series or in parallel).

Now the composite capacitor is connected to a DC voltage source $\mathcal{E}$ with internal resistance $r$. At the initial moment the capacitor is uncharged.

Find the time dependence of the current $I(t)$ flowing through the source. Express the answer in terms of $\mathcal{E}$, $r$, $R$, $C$ and $t$.

Part C. A capacitor with two dielectrics

A parallel-plate capacitor with plate separation $d$ is filled with two dielectrics. One has dielectric constant $\varepsilon_1$, resistivity $\rho_1$ and area of contact with the plates $S_1$, the other has dielectric constant $\varepsilon_2$, resistivity $\rho_2$ and area of contact with the plates $S_2$. Both dielectrics are in good electrical contact with the capacitor plates.

Find the equivalent capacitance $C_0$ of this capacitor. Express the answer in terms of $S_1$, $S_2$, $\varepsilon_1$, $\varepsilon_2$, $d$ and physical constants.

Find the equivalent resistance $R_0$ of this capacitor. Express the answer in terms of $S_1$, $S_2$, $\rho_1$, $\rho_2$ and $d$.

This capacitor, in series with a capacitor of capacitance $C$, is connected to a DC voltage source $\mathcal{E}$ with zero internal resistance. While the switch was open, the capacitors were uncharged. The voltage across the composite capacitor is measured by an ideal voltmeter $V$.

Find the charge $q_C(0)$ on the capacitor of capacitance $C$ immediately after the switch is closed. Express the answer in terms of $\mathcal{E}$, $C$ and $C_0$.

Find the voltmeter reading $V(t)$ as a function of time $t$ after the switch is closed. Express the answer in terms of $\mathcal{E}$, $C$, $C_0$, $R_0$ and $t$.

What amount of heat $Q_{R_0}$ is released in the resistance $R_0$? Express the answer in terms of $\mathcal{E}$, $C$ and $C_0$.
