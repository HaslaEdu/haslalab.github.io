In this problem you are to investigate how the length of the shadow of a light black ball, tied by a thread to the bottom of a wide vessel and illuminated by sunlight, depends on the level $h$ of the transparent liquid in the vessel. The length of the shadow is defined as the maximum distance between its boundary points. Assume that the Archimedes force acting on the ball in air is sufficient to keep the thread taut.

In what follows, use the following notation:

1. Length of the thread $l$

2. Radius of the ball $R$

3. Angle of incidence of the rays on the liquid surface $\alpha$

4. Angle of refraction of the rays $\beta$

5. Length of the shadow $L$

The graph shows the dependence of the shadow length on the liquid level in the vessel.

Part A. Before the kink

Express $L$ in terms of $R$ and $\alpha$ for $h = 0$.

Let $h_1$ be the water level in the vessel at the moment when the kink in the graph begins.

Express $L$ in terms of $R$, $\alpha$, $\beta$, $l$ and $h$ for $0 < h < h_1$.

Express $h_1$ in terms of $l$, $R$, $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

Part B. Study of the kink

Let $h_2$ be the water level at the moment when the kink turns into the linear part of the dependence.

For $h_1 < h < h_2$, obtain the dependence of the shadow length on $l$, $R$, $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $h$.

Express $h_2$ in terms of $l$, $R$, $\alpha$, $\beta$.

Part C. Linear part

Let $h_3$ be the liquid level at the moment when the shadow length stops changing.

Since this part is linear, the dependence $L(h)$ has the form $L = L_0 - kh$. Express $L_0$ and $k$ in terms of $l$, $R$, $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

Express the minimum shadow length in terms of $l$, $R$, $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

C

Express $h_3$ in terms of $l$, $R$, $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

Part D. Numerical values

Find the numerical values of $\cos\alpha$ and $\cos\beta$.

Find the radius $R$ of the ball.

Find the refractive index $n$ of the liquid.

Find the length $l$ of the thread.
